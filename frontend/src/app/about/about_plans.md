Founder story

Company vision and mission
- Empowering people everywhere with privacy-first health data that is useful, portable, and equitable — delivering clinician-grade insights to reduce disease burden and improve care for all.
- To help people with gastrointestinal diseases and their health care teams work together more effectively by making it effortless to track health data (symptoms, diet, medications, biomarkers), share it securely, and surface patterns that support timely, informed treatment decisions.
- Build a privacy-first platform that turns personal health data into trusted insights for patients, clinicians, and researchers worldwide.
- Create a world full of "Meadow Mentors", dieticians, nutritionists, health coaches who specialize in improving people's gut health.
