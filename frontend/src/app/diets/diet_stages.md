Here is the chart detailing the stages of these five diets.

**Important Note:** The Mediterranean Diet differs from the others as it is a lifestyle pattern rather than a clinical protocol with strict "stages." It is included here with its frequency tiers instead of phases.

| Diet Protocol | Stages / Phases | Duration | Allowed Foods & Texture | Goal of Stage | When to Move On |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Autoimmune Protocol (AIP)** | **Phase 1: Elimination** | 30–90 days (or until symptoms resolve) | **Foods:** Meat, fish, vegetables (except nightshades), fruit (limited), coconut/olive/avocado oil, bone broth, fermented foods.<br>**Avoid:** Grains, legumes, dairy, eggs, nuts, seeds, nightshades, alcohol, sugar, NSAIDs.<br>**Texture:** Normal, but focus on nutrient density. | Calm the immune system, heal the gut lining, and reduce inflammation. | When clear improvement in symptoms is achieved (no sooner than 30 days). |
| | **Phase 2: Reintroduction** | Varies (weeks to months) | Introduce one food at a time (e.g., egg yolks, then seeds). Wait 5–7 days between new foods to watch for reactions. | Identify specific food triggers that cause symptoms. | After testing a food, if no reaction occurs after 7 days, keep it in the diet and test the next one. |
| | **Phase 3: Maintenance** | Indefinite | Eat a personalized diet of all AIP-compliant foods plus successfully reintroduced foods. Continue avoiding identified triggers. | Long-term sustainable health without unnecessary restriction. | N/A (This is the lifestyle phase). |
| **2. GAPS Diet (Gut and Psychology Syndrome)** | **Introduction (6 Stages)** | 3–6 weeks total (approx. 3–5 days per stage) | **Texture:** Heavily focused on soups, stews, and soft foods.<br>**Stage 1:** Meat stock, boiled meat/fish, well-cooked veg, probiotic juice.<br>**Stage 2:** Add raw egg yolks, stews, ghee.<br>**Stage 3:** Add avocado, pancakes (nut flour), scrambled eggs, sauerkraut.<br>**Stage 4:** Add roasted meats, olive oil, fresh juices.<br>**Stage 5:** Add raw veg, apple puree.<br>**Stage 6:** Add raw fruit, honey. | Heal the gut lining ("seal the leak") using easily digestible, collagen-rich foods. | Move to the next stage when digestion is normal (no diarrhea, bloating, or gas). If symptoms return, go back a stage. |
| | **Full GAPS** | 18–24 months | Fresh meats, animal fats, fish, eggs, fermented foods, vegetables, fruit, nuts, honey. **No:** Grains, sugar, starchy veg, processed foods. | Restore the gut microbiome and immune system balance. | When you have had normal digestion and health for at least 6 months. |
| | **Reintroduction** | Months | Gradually reintroduce potatoes, fermented gluten-free grains, then sourdough, etc. | Transition to a wider, healthy whole-food diet. | N/A |
| **3. Mediterranean Diet** | **Tier 1: Daily (Base)** | Ongoing | Fruits, vegetables, whole grains, olive oil, beans, nuts, seeds, herbs, spices. | Provide the foundation of nutrition; high fiber and antioxidants. | N/A (Lifestyle). |
| | **Tier 2: Weekly (Moderate)** | Ongoing | Fish, seafood (at least 2x week), poultry, eggs, cheese, yogurt. | Protein and healthy fats without excess saturated fat. | N/A |
| | **Tier 3: Occasional (Rare)** | Ongoing | Red meats and sweets (sugar-sweetened beverages, desserts). | Limit inflammation and chronic disease risk. | N/A |
| **4. Specific Carbohydrate Diet (SCD)** | **Intro Diet** | 2–5 days (Maximum) | **Foods:** Chicken/beef broth, broiled fish/meat, dry curd cottage cheese, eggs, cooked carrots, gelatin, grape juice.<br>**Texture:** Soft, peeled, well-cooked. | Jump-start healing by starving out harmful bacteria (removing their food source). | When diarrhea subsides or 5 days have passed. |
| | **Stages 1–5 (Progressive)** | Individualized (weeks to months) | **Stage 1:** Peeled cooked fruit/veg (pears, apples), homemade yogurt.<br>**Stage 2–3:** Add nut milks, cooked green beans, meats.<br>**Stage 4–5:** Raw vegetables, raw fruit, dried legumes, fried foods.<br>**Illegal:** All grains, lactose, sucrose, starchy tubers. | Gradually increase fiber and food complexity as the gut heals. | Move up a stage when the current foods are tolerated well with no symptoms. |
| | **Maintenance** | Indefinite | All "Legal" SCD foods. Generally grain-free, sugar-free, starch-free. | Prevent bacterial overgrowth and maintain remission. | N/A |
| **5. Low-FODMAP Diet** | **Phase 1: Elimination** | 2–6 weeks | **Avoid:** High FODMAPs (Wheat, onion, garlic, legumes, high-fructose fruit, lactose).<br>**Eat:** Rice, potatoes, meat, fish, eggs, low-FODMAP fruit (berries, citrus), lactose-free dairy. | Calm the gut and achieve baseline symptom relief (reset). | When symptoms (bloating, gas, pain) have significantly improved/stabilized. |
| | **Phase 2: Reintroduction (Challenge)** | 6–8 weeks | Test individual FODMAP groups (e.g., honey for fructose, milk for lactose) one by one over 3 days, followed by a "washout" period. | Identify exactly which types of carbohydrates trigger your symptoms. | After testing all major FODMAP groups. |
| | **Phase 3: Personalization** | Indefinite | Eat as much variety as possible, avoiding *only* the specific high-FODMAP foods that caused reactions in Phase 2. | Long-term diet diversity and gut health management. | N/A |
