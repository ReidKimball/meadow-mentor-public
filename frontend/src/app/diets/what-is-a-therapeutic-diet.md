# What is a Therapeutic Diet?

**Quick Definition:**  
A therapeutic diet is a nutrition plan designed to manage specific medical conditions like IBD, IBS., or Celiac Disease. Unlike fad diets focused on weight loss, protocols like **SCD**, **AIP**, or **Low-FODMAP**) focus on removing inflammatory food triggers to reduce symptoms and support long-term gut healing using specific nutrient-dense whole foods.

## How It Works: The "Safety Filter" for Your Kitchen

Therapeutic diets don't just restrict food; they replace irritation with nourishment. They work by identifying specific ingredients that trigger your immune system, such as lactose, gluten, or specific carbohydrates, and swapping them for nutrient-dense, whole foods that your body can actually digest.

They may also require changing the texture of the food from solid to liquid or pureed for even easier digestibility.

The goal isn't just to eat healthy; it's to eat **safely**.

## Which Diet is Right for You?

Common therapeutic protocols include:

- **[Specific Carbohydrate Diet (SCD)](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=/diets/scd-diet-app):** A grain-free, lactose-free, and sugar-free protocol often used for **Crohn's** and **Colitis**.    
- **[Autoimmune Protocol (AIP)](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=/diets/aip-diet-app):** An elimination diet designed to calm the immune system by removing potential inflammatory triggers like nightshades, nuts, and seeds.    
- **[GAPS Diet](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=/diets/gaps-diet-app):** Focuses on gut lining repair using bone broths and fermented foods.

- **Low-FODMAP:** A temporary restriction diet ideal for managing **IBS** bloating and gas.
